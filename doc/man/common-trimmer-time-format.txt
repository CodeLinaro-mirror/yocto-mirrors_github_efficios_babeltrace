. Date/time, without a UTC offset:
+
[verse]
__YYYY__-__MM__-__DD__$$[$$ __HH__:__II__[:__SS__[.__NANO__]]]
__HH__:__II__[:__SS__[.__NANO__]]
+
Without a time of day, the time is midnight.

. Seconds from origin:
+
[verse]
$$[$$-]__SEC__[.__NANO__]

. https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc3339#section-5.6[RFC{nbsp}3339] date-time:
+
[verse]
__YYYY__-__MM__-__DD__T__HH__:__II__:__SS__$$[$$.__NANO__]__OFFSET__

With:

_YYYY_::
    4-digit year.

_MM_::
    2-digit month (January is `01`).

_DD_::
    2-digit day.

_HH_::
    2-digit hour (24-hour format).

_II_::
    2-digit minute.

_SS_::
    2-digit second.

_NANO_::
    Nanoseconds (up to nine digits).

_OFFSET_::
    UTC offset: `Z` (UTC), `+__HH__:__II__` (east of UTC), or
    `-__HH__:__II__` (west of UTC).
+
`z` and `-00:00` are equivalent to `Z`.

_SEC_:: Seconds since origin.
